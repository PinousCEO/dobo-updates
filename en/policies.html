Account and services

You can sign in with Google, GitHub, Kakao, or an email verification link. The enrollment and consent terms of the external login provider apply. If you have already registered with the same email, use the existing login method or an email verification link. You may not share your account with others or fraudulently claim free credits repeatedly.

Tasks using my CLI·API keys on the desktop and DOBO cloud tasks are separate. Provider subscriptions and API usage fees are subject to each provider's pricing and terms. Cloud tasks will open once they are ready to be provided.

Credits and Payments

Receive 1,200 credits at signup, then 1,200 base credits every 30 days when you access your account. Missed periods do not accumulate. Free credits expire after 30 days. Keeping projects open and reading existing results do not use credits.

Credits Plus auto-renews at $4.99/month and adds 1,200 credits valid through that billing period. It does not include exclusive feature access or a Research task. Cancel renewal in your account. One-time top-ups cost $4.99 for 300, $9.99 for 700, or $19.99 for 1,600 credits and last 365 days. Prices are charged in USD and include tax. Card issuer currency-conversion or international fees may apply separately.

Terminal, Auto Space, Research, and Present AI use shared credits. Each Terminal request costs 2 credits, each Auto Space AI response costs 5, voice input costs 2, Research investigation costs 60, Research AI management costs 2, and each Present AI image costs 20 credits. Credits are deducted upfront when an operation starts and restored if it fails or is canceled. Research service AI costs are included; costs and usage limits charged by CLI/API providers connected through other features are separate. Creating a Research task is free, and credits are deducted for each automatic or manual investigation. Automatic investigations run while the app is running; if you do not have enough credits, a new run will not start. Period-based and included tasks purchased previously retain the terms for their applicable period.

When converting an existing Research time-based Task to a per-execution charge, prepaid credits corresponding to the remaining-period ratio are restored once, with decimals truncated. Past executions are not billed retroactively. Per-execution credits are feature usage fees, not charges for measured API costs. One retry is available per cycle, and the total number of attempts for the entire period is limited to the scheduled execution count plus 20% (rounded up). Automatic credit renewal for additional tasks applies only when selected, and renewal stops when a task is paused or deleted. Research for missed cycles is not executed retroactively. When a payment is canceled, credits from that payment are reclaimed; if any credits have already been used, account verification may be required. Submit refund requests to Customer Support with the order number. No actual paid top-ups are provided before the official refund standards, subscription cancellation notice, and operator information are finalized.

Privacy Policy

Pinous Cor., Ltd (the “Company”) processes the personal information of DOBO website and desktop app users as follows.

1. Purposes, items, and methods of collection

The company processes personal information to the extent necessary for membership registration and contract performance. Where consent is required for optional processing, separate consent is obtained, and retention required by law is based on the applicable law.

  • Membership registration · login: We process the user identifier, name or nickname of the selected login provider, the email address verified by the provider (if provided), the DOBO account identifier, and the signup time. For email login, we use the email address entered. We do not collect external login passwords.
  • Authentication · service protection: Handles login sessions, authentication requests·expiration times, authentication nonces, request counts, and limit times. The connecting IP is processed during server communication, and a hash of the IP is used in the database for API request limits.
  • Plan · credits · work settlement: We process account identifier; payment, credit issuance, deduction, and refund history; task identifier, title, type, status, and time; and Research task title, schedule, period, renewal settings, and run history. Task titles may include part of the input.
  • When using payment features: We process order identifiers; product, currency, and amount; payment, refund, and subscription status; payment provider transaction identifiers; and usage periods. Card numbers and security codes are handled on the payment provider's screen and are not stored on DOBO account servers.
  • Inquiries · Exercise of rights: We process the email address and contact information provided by the user, inquiry details, necessary order and account identification information, and processing results. Do not include passwords, card numbers, or API secret keys in your inquiry.

Information is collected through direct input from members, authentication responses from the selected login provider, communication between the app and server, and responses from payment providers. Refusing to provide required information may limit access to the relevant login, payment, and account features.

2. Desktop storage and use of AI services

Task files, conversations, settings, and outputs are stored on the user's device. When an AI feature is run, prompts and the files, images, and context required for execution may be sent to the selected CLI or API provider, and that provider's policies and the settings selected by the user will apply. The company does not uniformly guarantee the provider's retention period or whether the data will be used for training.

DOBO account servers receive the task title and execution information described in item 1 above to manage entitlements and settle usage. Task titles and execution information are sent for settlement of Terminal·Auto Space·Present. For Research investigations and AI management, the user-entered topics and conditions, along with the necessary research context, are sent to DOBO servers and service AI providers, and the processing results may be stored on the servers. User AI API keys are not sent to the settlement server. Deleting files in the app or logging out alone does not delete all information stored on account servers or by AI providers.

3. Retention and use period

  • Account and login connection information: Retained until membership withdrawal and destroyed without delay after withdrawal processing. Information subject to legal retention is stored separately, limited to the necessary items.
  • Plan · work · credit history: Retained for as long as necessary to provide the service and settle accounts, then destroyed when you withdraw your membership. Only items required as transaction evidence are stored separately for the statutory period below.
  • Authentication information: The session is valid for 7 days after issuance, while one-time login and app connection requests are valid for 10 minutes. It becomes invalid upon logout, use, cancellation, or expiration. Expiration is distinct from the physical deletion of stored data, and expired data is destroyed according to the cleanup procedure. External login access tokens are used only for user verification and are not stored in the database.
  • Negative request limit information: Data is destroyed when the purpose of the restriction ends. The current restriction period is up to 24 hours per path, and expired rows are cleaned up when subsequent requests are processed.
  • Customer inquiry: General inquiries are destroyed one year after processing is complete. Records related to consumer complaints or disputes are retained according to the statutory retention periods below.
  • Backup: The release operating policy is a maximum 30-day rolling retention period. Items marked for deletion are removed when the backup expires, and previously processed deletion requests are reapplied upon restoration.

For transactions subject to the E-Commerce Act, contract, withdrawal, and payment/supply records are retained for 5 years each; consumer complaint/dispute resolution records for 3 years; and labeling/advertising records for 6 months. Retained data is limited to the records and minimum information needed to identify the parties to the transaction, and is used only for that purpose during the separate retention period.

4. Destruction procedures and methods

Once the purpose of processing has been achieved or the retention period has ended, the person in charge verifies whether legal retention is required and destroys the information without delay. Information subject to legal retention is separated from service usage information and access is restricted. Electronic materials are deleted so they cannot be recovered or reproduced; paper materials, if any, are shredded or incinerated. Account deletion includes signing out sessions and organizing the account and related work records.

5. Provision to third parties · entrusted processing · overseas transfer

The company does not sell personal information or provide it to third parties for advertising purposes. Disclosures requiring a legal basis or separate consent are made only after meeting the applicable requirements. External logins selected by members, the AI provider's own processing, and processing entrusted by the company are explained separately.

The current project implements Google, GitHub, and Kakao login, Resend authentication emails, and Stripe payment integration. The fact that they are implemented does not mean that an operating contract has been concluded with those providers or that they are actually in use. There is also a Toss Payments route for confirming existing KRW orders, but the company has confirmed that it has no history of online sales.

Notices to Finalize Before Release: Before providing the relevant feature, this policy will specifically disclose the legal name and contact information of the actual entrusted party, the outsourced tasks, hosting and backup locations, the items, countries, timing, and methods of transfers abroad, the recipient’s purposes and retention period, how to refuse the transfer, and the effects of refusal. We will not begin processing the relevant personal information until the required consent and notice procedures are in place. You may request to refuse an overseas transfer from the contact below; if you refuse, use of features requiring that transfer may be restricted.

Users may request access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of processing of their personal information, withdraw consent, or cancel their membership. ceo@pinous.co.kr or +82-10-2980-1349Please provide the request type and signup email. The company verifies whether you are the individual concerned or a lawful representative using the minimum necessary means, processes requests within the statutory deadline, or explains the reasons for restriction or refusal and how to object. Information subject to statutory retention obligations may not be deleted immediately. Representatives may exercise rights using documentation that verifies their lawful authorization.

If consent is required from a legal representative for processing the personal information of children under 14, we do not collect it until the legal representative's consent and verification procedures are completed. If we learn that it was collected without these procedures, we will take the necessary protective measures and proceed with deletion.

7. Automatically collected devices such as cookies

We use a cookie for keeping you logged in (7 days) and a cookie for confirming login requests (10 minutes). You can block or delete storage in your browser's privacy·cookie settings, but this may limit login functionality. The site may store display preferences, such as animation playback settings, in your browser. The current site code does not include ad-tracking cookies or external visitor analytics scripts.

8. Measures to ensure security

In its operating environment, the company applies encrypted communications, restrictions on access to personal information, separation of secret keys and data from public access paths, and protected backups and access management. The current authentication implementation includes hashed storage of session tokens, authentication cookies that browser scripts cannot read, request-origin verification, and rate limiting. The company provides personal information handling training for operations staff and maintains incident response procedures.

9. Automated decisions and pseudonymous information

The current service is not designed to provide fully automated decisions that have a significant impact on an individual's rights or obligations. Features for the company to separately process pseudonymized information for statistical or scientific research and similar purposes are also outside the current operating scope. If such processing is introduced, we will explain the applicable details and how to exercise your rights.
