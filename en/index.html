Frequently asked questions

A few answers before you get started with DOBO.

Can I get started for free?

Create an account without adding a card, and try the tools you need with your base credits.

Which AI accounts do I need?

Terminal, Auto Space and Present AI require a connected CLI or API account.

Research works without a separate API key.

How do I use Research?

Add topics you are interested in to collect research results. Read the key points and visit the original sources for more detail.

Where do I manage my account and payments?

In My account, you can check your credit balance and payment history, add credits and manage your subscription.

When will new features be added?

We plan to introduce one new feature each month.

Is LINGO available?

LINGO is in development. It will be available when it is ready.
